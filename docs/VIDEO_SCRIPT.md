# Demo video script (about 3 minutes)

Record after mainnet deployment. You need two accounts in QIE Wallet: **Jeweller** and **Customer**, both funded. Open a fresh demo plan first, since timers keep running. Rehearse once, because the timers are real.

**Before you press record:** open the plan page, connect as Customer, and have the explorer ready in another tab. Close other tabs and hide your bookmarks.

| Time | Screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Home page, top | "Millions of Indian families save for weddings through a jeweller's monthly gold plan. The money goes straight to the shop. In 2019, the owners of Goodwin Jewellers reportedly vanished, owing about 700 people 21 crore rupees." |
| 0:18 | Scroll to the cases, then back to the hero | "Tola is the same gold plan, with one change: the shop can't touch your money until you have your jewellery. This is live on QIE mainnet with small demo amounts." |
| 0:30 | Open the plan, as Customer. Point at the demo timer note | "On this demo plan, one month is one minute. Real plans use real months." |
| 0:38 | Click Join, confirm in the wallet. Point at the passbook row | "I pay my first instalment. QIE's gold oracle sets the price, so this locks a weight of gold at today's rate. It's on my passbook, and on the chain." |
| 0:58 | Switch wallet to Jeweller. Open Jeweller's counter. Click **Collect** | "Now I'm the jeweller, and I want that money. Collect." |
| 1:08 | **Refused card appears.** Click the explorer link, show the failed transaction | "Refused, by the chain. That's a real failed transaction anyone can check. The only thing that releases this money is the customer confirming they got their jewellery." |
| 1:25 | Back on the plan. Move the "What if gold moves" slider to +30% | "But if gold rises, who covers the customer's gain? The jeweller, with a bond. This slider is a simulation. Watch the required bond climb. On a real plan, the base numbers come straight from the oracle." |

I don't need permission to do anything about it. Anyone can flag it, and the contract checks the facts. Payments stop, customers can leave free, and the jeweller gets a repair window." |
| 2:05 | Show the countdown, **cut the wait in editing**, then click **Declare default** | "They don't repair it. So anyone can declare default." |
| 2:15 | Passbook: click **Claim your money**. Show the wallet balance, then the public record table | "And I get back every dollar I paid, plus my share of the bond for any gold gain. The jeweller had no way to stop this." |
| 2:32 | Second plan, already matured: click **I received my jewellery**, then as Jeweller, **Collect** succeeds | "And when things go right: I confirm at the counter, and the jeweller is paid. An honest shop loses nothing but the float, and gains something big brands get from their name: a promise a customer can check." |
| 2:50 | Home page. Show the live link | "Tola, on QIE. Your jeweller can't run away with your gold savings. Try it at this link." |

## Keep it honest on camera

- Say "demo" and "small amounts", and keep the timer note visible.
- Say "simulation" when moving the slider.
- Don't say the gold gain is guaranteed. Principal is. The gain is covered as far as the bond goes.
- Don't name QIE Pass as used.
